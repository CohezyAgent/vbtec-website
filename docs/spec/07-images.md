Global prompt suffix (append to every prompt):
> photorealistic editorial photograph, natural light, warm but not orange colour grade, slightly desaturated greens, shallow depth of field where natural, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens, authentic and unstaged feel, no text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks, no visible screens with readable text, realistic hands and faces

Faces: keep people natural, partly turned away or mid-action; no direct-to-camera "stock smiles". These are **placeholders** – replace with real customer photography (with consent) when available; never present a generated family as a real customer (projects/reviews keep their "Voorbeeld…" badges).
